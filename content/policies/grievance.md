We publish this policy in compliance with the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020, the Information Technology Act, 2000, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

## 12.1 How to raise a grievance

Write to **drsarthakpsychology@gmail.com** with:

- your full name and registered email address;
- your cohort and enrolment reference;
- a clear description of the issue and the outcome you are seeking; and
- any supporting documents, screenshots, or correspondence.

## 12.2 Our timelines

- **Acknowledgement:** within **forty-eight hours** of receipt.
- **Resolution:** ordinarily within **fifteen days**, and in any event within **one month** of receipt, as required under the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020.
- Where a matter requires longer, we will tell you why and give an expected timeline.

## 12.3 Escalation

If you are not satisfied with the outcome, you may escalate in writing to the Grievance Officer, who will conduct an independent review and respond within fifteen days.
