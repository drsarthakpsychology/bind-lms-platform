## 3.1 Fee structure

Course fees, applicable discounts, and instalment options are stated on the course page and in your enrolment confirmation email. Fees are quoted in Indian Rupees (INR).

Fees are exclusive of applicable taxes unless expressly stated otherwise. Where GST applies, it will be charged at the prevailing rate and reflected in your invoice.

## 3.2 Instalment plans

Where we offer an instalment plan, the schedule of amounts and due dates is confirmed to you in writing at enrolment. Instalments are a payment convenience only. **Opting for instalments does not reduce your total liability:** on confirming your seat, you become liable for the full course fee.

## 3.3 Late or missed payments

If an instalment is not received by its due date:

- we will send a reminder and allow a grace period of **seven days**;
- if the amount remains unpaid after the grace period, we may suspend your access to the learning platform, live sessions, and community channels until the dues are cleared;
- suspension does not extend your course duration, and sessions missed during suspension will not be repeated for you; and
- results, transcripts, and certificates will be withheld until all dues are settled in full.

## 3.4 Discounts, scholarships and fee waivers

Discounts and fee waivers are granted at our discretion, apply only to the cohort for which they are granted, are personal to the recipient, and cannot be transferred, deferred, encashed, or combined with other offers unless expressly stated.

Where a waiver was granted on the basis of information you provided, and that information is found to be inaccurate, we may withdraw the waiver and recover the balance fee.

## 3.5 Invoices

Invoices are issued to the name and details provided at enrolment. Please ensure these are accurate at the time of payment; we may be unable to amend an invoice after it is issued.
