✽ Due to reported weight loss in some patients in trials with epilepsy,
topiramate is commonly used to treat weight gain, especially in
patients with psychotropic drug-induced weight gain

✽ Weight loss in epilepsy patients is dose-related with more weight
loss at high doses (mean 6.5 kg or 7.3% decline) and less weight loss
at lower doses (mean 1.6 kg or 2.2% decline)

✽ Changes in weight were greatest in epilepsy patients who weighed
the most at baseline (>100 kg), with mean loss of 9.6 kg or 8.4%
decline, while those weighing <60 kg had only a mean loss of 1.3 kg
or 2.5% decline

✽ Long-term studies demonstrate that weight losses in epilepsy
patients were seen within the first 3 months of treatment and peaked at
a mean of 6 kg after 12–18 months of treatment; however, weight
tended to return to pretreatment levels after 18 months

✽ Some patients with psychotropic drug-induced weight gain may
experience significant weight loss (>7% of body weight) with
topiramate up to 200 mg/day for 3 months, but this is not typical, is not
often sustained, and has not been systemically studied
