Other Warnings/Precautions
Insomnia may be a symptom of a primary disorder, rather than a
primary disorder itself
Some patients may exhibit abnormal thinking or behavioral changes
similar to those caused by other CNS depressants (i.e., either
depressant actions or disinhibiting actions)
Some depressed patients may experience a worsening of suicidal
ideation
Use only with extreme caution in patients with impaired respiratory
function or obstructive sleep apnea
Zolpidem and zolpidem CR should only be administered at bedtime
Temporary memory loss may occur at doses above 10 mg/night
Rare angioedema has occurred with sedative hypnotic use and could
potentially cause fatal airway obstruction if it involves the throat,
glottis, or larynx; thus if angioedema occurs treatment should be
discontinued
Sleep driving and other complex behaviors, such as eating and
preparing food and making phone calls, have been reported in
patients taking sedative hypnotics; in some cases these behaviors
have resulted in serious injury or death, prompting the FDA to
require a black box warning
