• Specifically, oxcarbazepine and its active

carbamazepine
• The active metabolite MHD, also called

S-MHD (active) and 20% R-MHD (inactive)
• R, S-licarbazepine is also in clinical

• The active S enantiomer of licarbazepine is
another related compound in development
as yet another novel mood stabilizer
✽ Most significant risk of oxcarbazepine

occurring within the first 3 months of
treatment, and occurring in 2–3% of
patients
• Unknown if this risk is higher than for
carbamazepine
✽ Since SSRIs can sometimes also reduce
sodium due to SIADH (syndrome of

• By analogy with carbamazepine, could
theoretically be useful in chronic
