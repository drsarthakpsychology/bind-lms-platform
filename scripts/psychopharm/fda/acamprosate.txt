DRUG: acamprosate

DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION • Recommended dose: 666 mg (two 333 mg tablets) taken three times daily ( 2 ). • Dose reduction to one 333 mg tablet taken three times daily for patients with moderate renal impairment (creatinine clearance 30-50 mL/min) ( 2.1 ). • Acamprosate calcium delayed-release tablets are contraindicated in patients with severe renal impairment (creatinine clearance of ≤ 30 mL/min) ( 2.1 , 4.2 , 5.1 , 8.6 , 12.3 ). DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS Enteric-coated tablets, 333 mg ( 3 ). CONTRAINDICATIONS • Acamprosate calcium delayed-release tablets are contraindicated in patients who previously have exhibited hypersensitivity to acamprosate calcium or any of its components ( 4.1 ). • Acamprosate calcium delayed-release tablets are contraindicated in patients with severe renal impairment ( 4.2 ). WARNINGS AND PRECAUTIONS • Dose reduction is required for patients with moderate renal impairment ( 5.1 ). • Monitor patients for depression or suicidal ideation and prompt patients, families, and caregivers to report such symptoms to the health care provider ( 5.2 ). ADVERSE REACTIONS Common adverse events that occurred in any acamprosate calcium delayed-release tablets treatment group at a rate of 3% or greater and greater than the placebo group in controlled clinical trials with spontaneously reported adverse events are: accidental injury, asthenia, pain, anorexia, diarrhea, flatulence, nausea, anxiety, depression, dizziness, dry mouth, insomnia, paresthesia, pruritus and sweating ( 6.1 ).
