DRUG: eszopiclone

DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION • Use the lowest dose effective for the patient ( 2 ) • Recommended initial dose is 1 mg, immediately before bedtime, with at least 7 to 8 hours remaining before the planned time of awakening. May increase dose if clinically indicated, to a maximum of 3 mg ( 2.1 ) • Geriatric or debilitated patients: Dose should not exceed 2 mg ( 2.2 ) • Patients with severe hepatic impairment, or taking potent CYP3A4 inhibitors: Dose should not exceed 2 mg ( 2.3 ) • Do not take with or immediately after a meal ( 2.5 ) DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS Tablets: 1 mg, 2 mg, and 3 mg ( 3 ) CONTRAINDICATIONS • Patients who have experienced complex sleep behaviors after taking eszopiclone tablets ( 4 ) • Known hypersensitivity to eszopiclone ( 4 ) WARNINGS AND PRECAUTIONS • CNS Depressant Effects Impaired alertness and motor coordination, including risk of morning impairment. Risk increases with dose and use with other CNS depressants and alcohol. Caution patients taking 3 mg dose against driving and against activities requiring complete mental alertness during the morning after use.
