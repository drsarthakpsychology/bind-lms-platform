## Full description (max 4000 characters)

```
Psychology you can practise.

Vibha Psychology is the app of VIBHA School of Psychology, a practical training programme in clinical psychology led by a practising psychiatrist in Ahmedabad. Theory gives you the language; practice teaches you how to use it.

The app is for our enrolled students. Sign in with the account the school gave you. If you are not a student yet, you can request an invitation from the app.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

Lectures
Watch every recorded lecture, at full quality. Pick up where you left off, read the lecture materials, and hand in assignments as text, files or a voice recording.

Study modules
Read each module in order, with hand-drawn diagrams and pieces you can tap and move. Check yourself at every checkpoint and finish with the module test.

Doubts
Ask a question about anything you studied. Your teachers and classmates answer, and the best answer is marked.

Practise
Short games and patient cases to keep what you learnt fresh.

Library
Look up common psychiatric medicines: what each group is for and the side effects to watch for.

Progress and certificates
See how far you have come, and keep your certificates in one place.

Notifications
Messages from the school, answers to your doubts and news about your certificates. You choose which ones you get.

MADE FOR STUDYING

- One account, the same as on vibhapsychology.com.
- A 4-digit PIN each time you open the app.
- Your account is open on one phone at a time.
- Screenshots and screen recording are blocked, to protect the course material.
- Dark mode and larger text follow your phone's settings.

No ads. No payments in the app.

Questions? Write to drsarthakpsychology@gmail.com or call +91 78770 49920.
```
