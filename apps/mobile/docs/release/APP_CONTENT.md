# Google Play "App content" answers

Play Console > your app > **Policy and programs** (left menu) > **App content**. Each card below is one box on that page. Click **Start** (or **Manage**) on it and pick what is written here. Data safety has its own page: DATA_SAFETY.md.

## Privacy policy

Paste: `https://vibhapsychology.com/policies/privacy` > **Save**.

## App access

- Pick **All or some functionality in my app is restricted**.
- Click **Add instructions**. Fill in:
  - Name: `Student account`
  - Username: the email of the reviewer account you made (OWNER_STEPS.md, step 4.1)
  - Password: its password
  - Any other information to access your app: paste this:

```
Sign in with the email and password above. The app then asks you to choose a 4-digit PIN: choose any PIN that is not easy to guess, for example 2580. Accounts are made by the school; there is no sign-up. Lecture videos do not play on rooted phones or emulators that report root; every other screen works there.
```
