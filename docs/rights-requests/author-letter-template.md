# Author permission letter — template

A short, warm letter asking an author for **their own consent**, independently of their publisher. We ask authors directly because AI training rights sit with the author, not automatically with the publisher — see `readme.md` for the reasoning (the Authors Guild's position). The publisher letter and the author letter are sent separately; neither is a substitute for the other.

## Letter body

[Date]

Dear [Author],

I'm [name], and I run **Casebook**, a training programme in India for early-career counselling and mental-health practitioners. Our students are learning the craft of the clinical conversation — how to listen, how to ask, when to refer. They practise with simulated patients in a secure application, and everything they meet there is built from the best clinical writing we know.

Your book, **[title]**, is one of the works that matters to us. I'd like to ask for your personal consent to use it as background reading for the programme — to index it into our internal corpus and let the application learn from it, so our teaching materials are grounded in genuine clinical insight rather than guesswork.

Two things I want to state plainly, because they're at the heart of how we work:

1. **We never serve your text to students.** Nothing a student sees is reproduced from your book. Everything is written fresh — original wording *derived* from what your book teaches — with attribution to you.
2. **We are also asking your publisher separately** for the institutional and licensing side of this. The author's consent and the publisher's consent are separate rights, and we will not proceed without yours.

We'd like to thank you properly — an attribution in the programme's credits, and a modest honorarium of [amount] for the personal consent.

I'd be delighted to show you the application, answer any questions, or adjust any of this. If you'd rather not consent, that's entirely your call, and I'll be glad to leave your work out of the corpus.

With respect and thanks,

[Your name]
[Role], Casebook
[Email] · [Phone]
[City], India

## Checklist before sending

- [ ] Title and edition correct
- [ ] Plain statement that no verbatim text is served
- [ ] Plain statement that publisher consent is sought separately
- [ ] Attribution and fee offer included
- [ ] Sent to the author's own address (via their publisher or agent if you only have that route)
