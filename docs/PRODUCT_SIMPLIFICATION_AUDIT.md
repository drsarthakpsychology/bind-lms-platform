# Product Simplification Audit (T91)

_Product simplification reset — full application-wide audit. Question: **"Would a student or admin immediately understand this without reading an explanation?"**_

- **Date:** 2026-08-14 · **Method:** 13 surface clusters audited by read-only agents (64 agents, 2.37M tokens), every priority-1 finding re-verified by an independent skeptic re-read.
- **Result:** 165 findings · 51 priority-1 (46 confirmed / 4 refuted / 1 verified manually) · 71 priority-2 · 43 priority-3.
- **Operating rule:** per Kavya — "DO BEST, not safest-to-reverse." Fixes are the best product call, gated and committed.

## The five recurring problems
Ranked by how often they appeared across surfaces:

1. **jargon** (57 × 35%) — engineering/database/product terms shown to students and admins
2. **internal-architecture-exposed** (47 × 28%) — DB status enums, provider names, raw IDs, route paths, key/config details leaked into the UI
3. **duplication** (20 × 12%) — same number/concept shown twice; two names for one thing; repeated content blocks
4. **over-explanation** (18 × 11%) — helper paragraphs, decorative subtitles, design-rationale copy that don't drive a decision
5. **confusing-navigation** (12 × 7%) — labels that don't describe the destination; dead-end links
6. **cognitive-load** (4 × 2%) — too many simultaneous choices; no clear hierarchy
7. **competing-actions** (4 × 2%) — two or more visually equal primary actions; ambiguous next step
8. **dead-feature** (2 × 1%) — disabled buttons, empty banks, surfaces with no content behind them
9. **desktop-density** (1 × 1%) — tables/dense grids not reworked for phone

## Findings by surface
Legend: **P1** = fix now · **P2** = next · **P3** = later / needs a product decision. Confirmed = a skeptic re-read confirmed the finding. Refuted = the skeptic showed it was a false positive. Action: remove / merge / rename / hide.
