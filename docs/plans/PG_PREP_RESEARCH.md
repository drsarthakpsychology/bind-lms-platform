## How to read this doc

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **OFFICIAL** | Read in a document from the conducting body (NTA, UGC, RCI, the institute or its university) |
| **SECONDARY** | From a coaching site, news report or third-party summary. Treat as likely but unconfirmed |
| **UNVERIFIED** | No source found that we could read, or sources conflict. Do not show students as fact |
| **VIBHA DESIGN** | Our own planning choice, not a fact about the exam |

Rules we kept:
- No exam pattern or number is stated without a source tag.
- Patterns change every cycle. The platform must show "as reported for the {year} cycle; check the official notice" next to every pattern.
- Many official PDFs could not be downloaded directly. We read them through a text extractor. Where an extractor could only give a summary (for example the UGC NET syllabus), that is stated.

## 1. CUET PG Psychology (HUQP20)

### 1.1 Pattern (2026 cycle), OFFICIAL

| Mode | Computer-based test only (§2.1) |

| Duration | 90 minutes (§2.4) |

- The 2027 cycle notice had not been seen as of 6 Nov 2026 (UNVERIFIED). Careers360 already labels HUQP20 as "Psychology, Applied Psychology, Health Psychology etc." under a 2027 syllabus page (SECONDARY).
- Pace: 72 seconds per question.
- Break-even for guessing at +4/−1 with four options is 1 in 5. A blind guess (1 in 4) has a small positive expected value: +0.25 marks. Eliminating one option raises it to about +0.67, and eliminating two raises it to +1.5.

### 2.5 Question styles, observed
Seen in compilations of past NET Psychology items ([Fatskills NET Psychology PYQs](https://www.fatskills.com/ugc-net/quiz/ugc-net-psychology-previous-papers-questions), SECONDARY), and consistent with NTA's post-2018 format:

| Style | Example stem from past items |
|---|---|
| Assertion (A) / Reason (R) | "A: Encoding specificity states a retrieval cue works only if present at encoding. R: …" |
| Two statements (Statement I / II) | "Given below are two statements … choose the most appropriate answer" |
| Match List I with List II | Theorists ↔ theories; tests ↔ constructs; brain areas ↔ functions |
| Multi-statement ("A, B and D only") | "Which of the following are non-parametric tests?" |
| Arrange in sequence | Stages of Piaget, Kübler-Ross, steps of test construction |
| Research-method calculations | SEM, df, Spearman-Brown, z-scores, effect size, choosing the right test |
| Passage-based sets | Paper 1 RC and DI for certain. Paper 2 passages are reported in older papers; current use UNVERIFIED |

What this means for students (as of Nov 2026):
- **Programme name:** M.Clin.Psy (RCI), 2 years. It leads to registration in RCI's Central Rehabilitation Register as a Clinical Psychologist.
- **Typical 2026 eligibility** (NIMHANS, CHRIST, LGBRIMH): a 4-year BA/BSc Psychology, or a 3-year degree **plus** a bridge course **or** the first year of an MA/MSc Psychology, with ≥ 55%. NIMHANS also accepts a full Master's in Psychology if psychology was a core subject in every UG year (SECONDARY for NIMHANS; OFFICIAL for LGBRIMH, CHRIST).
- Some 2025 cycles (CIP, RINPAS, IHBAS, GMCH) still ran as **M.Phil** and required a full MA/MSc. Whether any M.Phil intake ran for 2026 is UNVERIFIED and likely institution-specific.
- **PsyD:** described as another RCI route. It is a 4-year programme reported at Amity Noida, Amity Jaipur, NIEPVD Dehradun and MAN College Guna; eligibility MA/MSc ≥ 65% (60% SC/ST); Amity also asks for a dissertation and two recommendation letters ([UPS 2022, modified Aug 2026](https://www.upseducation.in/update/psyd-clinical-psychology-in-india-colleges-rci-license-books-ups-education/amp/), SECONDARY). Entrance pattern and interview: not published (UNVERIFIED).
- RCI-approved M.Clin.Psy list: a secondary list modified on 5 Nov 2026 names 66 institutions, including NIMHANS, CIP, RINPAS, LGBRIMH, IHBAS, IOP Kolkata, University of Calcutta, CHRIST, the Amity campuses, Manipal, DMC Ludhiana, St John's, DIMHANS and others. **No AIIMS and no PGIMER** appear ([UPS list](https://www.upseducation.in/update/rci-approved-ma-clinical-psychology-colleges-in-india/amp), SECONDARY). Confirm against RCI's own list on [rehabcouncil.nic.in](https://rehabcouncil.nic.in/document-category/circulars-notifications/).

**Typical question themes** (compiled from the sources above plus common practice in Indian clinical training interviews; **UNVERIFIED as a list**, so use it for practice only):
- **Academic core:** define and contrast concepts (reliability vs validity, psychosis vs neurosis, DSM-5-TR vs ICD-11); research methods and statistics (choose a test for a design; Type I/II error; power).
- **Clinical:** a short vignette → differential diagnosis, the MSE findings you would look for, which test you would use and why, risk assessment.
- **Testing practical:** name, purpose, age range and scoring of common tests (WAIS/MISIC, Binet-Kamat, VSMS, Bhatia battery, PGI Memory Scale, Bender-Gestalt, Rorschach, TAT, MMPI, 16PF, NIMHANS neuropsychological battery). Some panels ask candidates to explain or administer a test (reported, UNVERIFIED).
- **Research:** your MA dissertation (question, design, sample, analysis, limitations); a proposal idea.
- **Ethics and law:** confidentiality limits, consent and capacity, MHCA 2017 (advance directive, nominated representative, decriminalised suicide attempt, section 115), RPwD Act 2016, RCI Act 1992 and the licensing requirement.
- **Motivation and fit:** why clinical psychology, why this institute, career plan, handling distress and supervision.
- **Current affairs:** Tele-MANAS, the National Mental Health Programme, NEP 2020 and the M.Phil → M.Clin.Psy change, the National Suicide Prevention Strategy (2022), the DSM-5-TR and ICD-11 updates.
